This is not really an article. I just want a clean place to keep the coffee
recipes I make all the time, so I don't have to work out the numbers again.

This one is my favorite Japanese iced coffee: brewed hot with the Kalita Wave,
directly over ice. It is quick, bright, and cold as soon as the brew is done.

## The recipe

|                 | Amount                            |
| --------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Coffee          | **32.5 g**                        |
| Total water     | **500 g**                         |
| Ice             | **200 g** (40%)                   |
| Hot water       | **300 g** (60%)                   |
| Ratio           | **65 g per liter** (about 1:15.4) |
| Grind           | Finer than my usual Kalita grind  |
| Bloom           | **45 seconds**                    |
| Total brew time | **2–3 minutes**                   |

## How I make it

1. Put **200 g of ice** in the server and place the Kalita Wave on top.
2. Grind **32.5 g of coffee**, a little finer than I would for a normal hot
   Kalita.
3. Bloom with part of the hot water for **45 seconds**.
4. Keep pouring as usual until I reach **300 g of hot water**. I aim to finish
   the whole brew somewhere between **2 and 3 minutes**.
5. Remove the brewer and stir the coffee and ice with a spoon.

That's it. **32.5 g coffee, 200 g ice, 300 g hot water.** :)

The useful part to remember is the split: **40% ice and 60% hot water**. For a
different batch size, I keep the coffee at **65 g per liter** and use the same
40/60 split.
